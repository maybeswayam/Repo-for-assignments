Charmandr

Charmander is a Fire-type Pokémon introduced
in Generation I. The flame that burns at the tip of
its tail is an indication of its emotions.
The flame wavers when Charmander is enjoying itself.

Bulbasur

Bulbasaur is a Grass/Poison-type Pokémon.
For some time after its birth, it grows by
gaining nourishment from the seed on its back.
It carries the seed from birth.

Squirtel

Squirtle is a Water-type Pokémon.
After birth, its back swells and hardens into a shell.
It powerfully sprays foam from its mouth.
Shoots water at prey while in the water.

Jigglypuf

Jigglypuff is a Normal/Fairy-type Pokémon.
When its huge eyes waver, it sings a mysteriously
soothing melody that lulls its enemies to sleep.
It has a huge lung capacity.
